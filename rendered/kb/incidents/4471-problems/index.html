5 of these read differently from the cause they are filed under: the desk noted them as one-day moves, not month/day swaps. They stay counted under parser, where the desk files them, and the note travels with the ticket.

parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant writes the day first, so the month and the day swap. This is the fault #4471 was opened for, and the one the queued fix addresses.

hint — through the booking-v3 form, the tenant’s people type what the printed hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The dates still land on the wrong day, but no shared-parser change touches them: the form’s hint is what is wrong here.

The hint ones sit inside #4471 but outside its fix. The parser fix does not reach them, so they need a ticket of their own.

Two numbers in the export that do not survive the data

  • One id, one report. The ticket system exports in overlapping pages, so the same report can arrive twice. The counts above come from the reports themselves, one id each.
  • meta.intakeTag is a guess. It is what intake guessed from the reporter’s browser at the moment the report came in, and it disagrees with the cause a report is filed under here — the “tagged as” column shows how far. The tenant, its booking form and the channel decide the cause. The tag does not.

The ones that read differently

Five of the parser ones do not read like month/day swaps. The desk caught them by hand and wrote them down in desk-notes.json: a day moves, and an invite arrives before the booking was made. They stay counted under parser, where the tenant rule files them and where the desk’s own table counts them, and the note travels with that ticket: a month/day fix will not move a date that is one day out, so they need looking at on their own.

Where #4471 ended up

The engineering update, 05 October:

  • parser — fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint — won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired.

#4471 is closed. The parser ticket closes as completed on that evidence. The hint ticket closes as not planned, because the affected form was retired, not because the hint was fixed.
