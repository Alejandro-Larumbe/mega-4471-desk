owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-11-03 · 0 days ago

Date: 3 November 2026 · Deciders: support desk

Context

The desk merged 412 reports about dates into one ticket, #4471, and marked it known: one issue, one fix queued, the parser reading dates month first while every tenant on file writes the day first. The merge was made on subject line alone (decision 0001), so a second defect rode in with the first one and sat there counted under the same number.

Read by cause instead of by subject, the pile holds two problems, and the desk’s own cause table already says so: it marks the hint group “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”.

Decision

One ticket per problem, counted the way the desk counts:

problemreportsticketoutcome
parser379#4closed as completed
hint33#5closed as not planned

The parser reports stay on #4471 and keep its queued fix. The hint reports get a ticket of their own, because the form’s printed hint is what is wrong there and no shared-parser change reaches it.

The five reports the desk read as one-day moves stay counted under parser, where the tenant rule files them and where the desk’s own table counts them. They are footnoted on that ticket, not split off: a month/day fix will not move a date that is one day out, so they need looking at on their own.

Engineering update

#4471 was closed on 05 October:

  • parser — fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint — won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired.

The parser ticket closes as completed on that evidence. The hint ticket closes as not planned, because the affected form was retired, not because the hint was fixed.

Consequences

  • The queue stops being one number. 379 + 33 = 412.
  • Count by cause, from the reports themselves, one id each. The export pages overlap, and meta.intakeTag is a guess about the reporter’s browser, not the cause.
  • The signed page for the split is the desk’s own page for these two problems.
The count a problem is given comes from the cause rule, never from how the reports were tagged or from how many rows the export shipped.
